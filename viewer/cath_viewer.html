Window

Channels ?

Scale = full-scale range (mV for ECG, mmHg for pressure). Scroll the wheel over a lane to change it; the box here follows. Drag the bold line between the ECG and pressure panes to give either one more height. Overlay uses the arterial channel's scale.

Study range ?

Keys: I = in, O = out. Dragging a handle on the track moves the waveform display with it, so you can see exactly where you are cutting.

Pressure cleaning ?

These limits cut data. Samples outside them are voided in the export. The lane scale below the channel list only changes the drawing.

ECG cleaning

Remove wander subtracts a running-median estimate of the drifting baseline. Median window must stay longer than a QRS (≈0.1 s) or it starts tracking the beat and flattens the R wave.

Frequency

Off by default: this recorder has no mains peak and no power above 40 Hz.

Export

Same settings, whole file, much faster:

clean_export.py <stem>

Open a recording

Select the .inf and .bin for a case (both at once), or drag them here.

Nothing is uploaded. The file is read straight off your disk in this browser tab, a few seconds at a time, so a 500 MB recording opens instantly.
